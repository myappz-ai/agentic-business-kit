# Industry packs – starting points for the organic engine

The method (3 posts a day, 10 slides, gift on slide 4, comment-to-DM, nightly score) is the same for every business and is **proven**. These packs only change *what the posts talk about*, so a new business never starts from a blank page.

## How to use a pack
1. In Phase 0, match the business to a pack from its profile (industry + what it sells). If two packs fit, or the "Ask" line changes the plan, ask the owner one question.
2. Copy the pack's pillars, topic starters, free-resource ideas and keywords into the brand's Topic Bank. Rewrite them for this brand's audience, city and offer. Research every fact; the packs contain no facts or numbers.
3. Save the brand's own choices as its style (see "The brand's own style" in SKILL.md). From day one the nightly review learns from the brand's own numbers and can replace anything here.
4. The DM always gives the free resource first. The resource page ends with the pack's **next step**, which matches the industry's default funnel chain.

## Status of each pack
- **Starter**: a sensible first plan, not yet proven with numbers. Every pack starts here.
- **Proven**: beat the brand's 30-post median on at least 2 businesses in that industry over 30+ posts. Record the evidence under the pack (accounts, dates, median vs result) when you promote it.

## Slots for consumer businesses
For B2C packs, the 20:00 "sales and growth" slot becomes **buying help**: how to choose, what it costs and why, mistakes to avoid, before/after stories with consent. Same slot, same purpose: comments from people ready to buy.

### Training & coaching — Starter
- Ask: live cohort, recorded course or 1:1?
- Pillars: 08:00 the skill itself · 13:00 learning, discipline, mindset · 20:00 career or income growth from the skill (no income promises)
- Topic starters: "7 mistakes beginners make in <skill>"; "7 free tools to practise <skill>"; "7 habits of people who finish courses"; "7 signs you're ready for the next level"; "7 questions to ask before joining any course"
- Free resources: practice plan, starter checklist, cheat sheet, 30-day roadmap
- Keywords: PLAN, START, ROADMAP, SKILL
- Next step: webinar or VSL → 1:1 call
- Never: income or job guarantees, "become an expert in 7 days"
