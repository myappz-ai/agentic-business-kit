# Content rules – carousels, captions, keywords

These rules are proven on the reference account (Nov 2026). The content changes per brand; the rules do not.

## What the data showed (reference account, last 30 posts)
- Posts that asked for a comment keyword had a median reach of 2,497, against 695 for posts that didn't.
- The 4 posts with the most comments (1,778 / 783 / 415 / 207) all asked for the comment **in the first lines of the caption**.
- **Lists beat stories.** A "7 ideas" list carousel got 36 saves and 11 shares. Single-story carousels got 1–3 saves.
- The winners name a specific tool or outcome, or show money or time saved. Vague "growth" posts got 2–56 comments.

## Slots (default; adjust only with the owner)
| Slot (brand TZ) | Style | Pillar | Built for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 08:00 | v3 white post-card | The brand's core expertise | Saves and comments |
| 13:00 | v5 cream big-type | Universal: life, work, learning, wisdom, history, told for the brand's audience | Shares and saves |
| 20:00 | v3 white post-card | Sales and growth for the audience | Comments |

## Carousel pattern (10 slides by default)
1. **Hook**: "7 …" plus a promise of time, money, or "quietly changes how you…". Add "Swipe →" and the line **"🎁 Free gift on slide 4 →"**.
2–3. **The first list items**, one per slide, numbered.
4. **Gift preview**: the free resource's title, 3 short teasers of what's inside, a FREE badge, and "Comment KEYWORD and I'll send you the <doc>". The preview must match the real resource page, so build that page first.
5–8. **The rest of the list**, one item per slide.
9. **"Screenshot this" recap** of all 7 items.
10. **CTA**: "Comment KEYWORD and I'll send you the free <doc>", plus one save line and one share line ("Send this to a friend who…").
- Hook test: write 3 cover headlines. Pick the one that promises a result or creates tension.
- Run the renderer QA every time. If something overflows, shorten the copy; never shrink the type.

## Caption
- Line 1: the hook.
- **Line 2: "Comment KEYWORD and I'll send you the free <doc>."** It must sit above the "more" fold. Never replace it with an open question, and always ask for a comment (never "DM me"): comments trigger the flow and lift reach.
- Then 3–4 value lines.
- Repeat the CTA once at the end, then add a save line, a share line, a `Source:` line (when facts are used) and 3–5 hashtags.
- Under 2,200 characters.

## Words for the free resource
- Rotate across posts: gift, doc, cheat sheet, guide, checklist, playbook.
- Inside one post, use the same word everywhere: slide 1, slide 4, slide 10, the caption, the reply and the DM.

## Keyword
- One common word, 3–8 letters, letters only, uppercase. Easy to type on a phone. Good: AGENT, JAPAN, PRICE, SLEEP. Bad: GROWTH2026, AI-TOOLS, TIPS, INFO.
- The same word goes in the caption, on slides 4 and 10, and as the flow's comment trigger.
- **Reuse is allowed.** The comment keyword is matched per post, so JAPAN on post 1 and JAPAN on post 5 each send their own resource.
  - Give each resource its own address: `japan`, then `japan-2`, `japan-3`.
  - DM keywords are account-wide. Set the DM keyword only when no other active flow uses that word (check `list_social_flows`); otherwise leave it empty.
  - Log every keyword with its post, resource address and date.

## Tone (LOCKED)
- Never put the reader down. Challenge ideas, never people. No "you are the problem", no shaming, no "lazy".
- Uplifting and shareable. End on hope plus one small action.

## Facts (LOCKED)
- Facts come only from pages actually opened during research (search, then open). Add a Source line.
- No invented numbers. No income or result promises. No "first-ever". No fake discounts.
- Never feature the brand's competitors. For a software or agency brand, that means no rival CRM, booking, inbox, email-marketing or WhatsApp tools.

## Rejected styles (do not use)
- Illustrated or drawn carousels.
- Photo-strip carousels (a stock photo on top with a small text card below).
- Use only the v3 white post-card and the v5 cream big-type, branded with the owner's name, photo and title.

## Topic Bank
- Keep at least 5 unused topics per slot. Each one gets a planned KEYWORD and a guide idea.
- Start from the brand's own best posts: anything above 2× the median reach becomes a topic or a "Part 2".
- Mark topics `USED <date> (<shortcode>)`.

## Virality pattern (what the nightly review protects)
- Lists beat stories; specific beats vague.
- The comment ask sits in the first two caption lines and on slides 1, 4 and 10.
- Saves and shares come from the recap slide, the save line and the share line. Never drop them.
- Score = reach + 3×comments + 3×shares + 2×saves + likes, against the 30-post median. Winners get a "Part 2" with a new keyword; losers' formats are dropped.
