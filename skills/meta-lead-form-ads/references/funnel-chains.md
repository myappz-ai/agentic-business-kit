# Industry → default funnel chain

Recommend the default chain first and offer the alternate. Ask what kind of business it is only when the answer changes the flow (gym vs yoga studio, clinic vs hospital, packages vs custom trips).

| Industry | Default chain | Alternate |
|---|---|---|
| Insurance | Meta lead form (lowest CPL) → tele-calling → virtual call or home visit → policy | Workshop → 1:1 |
| Financial services (loans, investments) | Ad → lead form → tele-calling → meeting → application | — |
| Education | Lead form → tele-calling → campus visit → admission | Direct campus-visit booking |
| Travel | Ad → lead form → tele-calling (packages) → proposal → booking | Ad → consultation booking (custom trips) |
| Real estate | Ad → webinar (projects) → 1:1 virtual call → site visit → close | Lead form → tele-calling → site visit |
| Architects / interiors | Ad → lead form → tele-calling → virtual or site meeting → proposal | — |
| Distributors | Ad to retailers → lead form → meeting → dealer agreement | — |
| Fitness | Ad → free trial / demo class booking → visit → membership | Lead → tele-calling → invite |
| Healthcare (clinic) | Ad → lead or booking → appointment → reminder call → consultation | — |
| Beauty (high-ticket: bridal, makeup) | Enquiry → tele-calling → consult → booking | Direct booking (regular services) |
| Training & coaching | Ad → VSL or webinar → 1:1 call → enrol | Tele-calling |
| Consulting | Ad → VSL → application / call → proposal | — |
| Events | Ad → enquiry → meeting → proposal | Own summit / expo |
| Hiring (any industry) | Ad → lead form → screening call → interview | — |

Lead forms fit best where a person must call or meet the lead before a sale. For direct booking or shop funnels, use a calendar or landing page instead of an instant form.

## Built into every lead-form chain
- **Speed to lead:**
  - Instant WhatsApp (utility template) and email on submit.
  - A call task assigned to an owner, due in 5 minutes.
  - An alert to the owner.
- **Show-up** (for any appointment step):
  - Confirmation, a 24 h reminder, a 1 h reminder and a no-show follow-up.
  - Suggest a paid or deposit booking where no-shows are high.
- **Nurture:** a day 1 / 3 / 7 follow-up for leads who don't pick up.
