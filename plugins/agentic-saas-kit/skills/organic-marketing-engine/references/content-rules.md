# Content rules – carousels, captions, keywords

These rules are proven on the reference account (Nov 2026). The content changes per brand; the rules do not.

## What the data showed (reference account, last 30 posts)
- Posts that asked for a comment keyword had a median reach of 2,497, against 695 for posts that didn't.
- The 4 posts with the most comments (1,778 / 783 / 415 / 207) all asked for the comment **in the first lines of the caption**.
- **Lists beat stories.** A "7 ideas" list carousel got 36 saves and 11 shares. Single-story carousels got 1–3 saves.
- The winners name a specific tool or outcome, or show money or time saved. Vague "growth" posts got 2–56 comments.

## Carousel pattern (10 slides by default)
1. **Hook**: "7 …" plus a promise of time, money, or "quietly changes how you…". Add "Swipe →" and the footer "Free guide at the end →".
2. **Why it matters**: can be merged into the hook slide's body to save a slide.
3–9. **One item per slide**, numbered.
10. **CTA**: "Comment KEYWORD and I'll DM you the free <guide>", plus one save line and one share line ("Send this to a friend who…").
- Add a "screenshot this" recap slide before the CTA when the slides allow.
- Hook test: write 3 cover headlines. Pick the one that promises a result or creates tension.
- Run the renderer QA every time. If something overflows, shorten the copy; never shrink the type.

## Caption
- Line 1: the hook.
- **Line 2: "Comment KEYWORD and I'll DM you the free <guide>."** It must sit above the "more" fold. Never replace it with an open question.
- Then 3–4 value lines.
- Repeat the CTA once at the end, then add a save line, a share line, a `Source:` line (when facts are used) and 3–5 hashtags.
- Under 2,200 characters.

## Keyword
- One word, uppercase, specific to the post, never reused (check the Log). Good: AGENT, SEARCH, JAPAN, WHATSAPP. Bad: GROWTH, TIPS, INFO.
- The same word goes in the caption, on the CTA slide, as the flow trigger and as the guide's slug (lowercase).

## Tone (LOCKED)
- Never put the reader down. Challenge ideas, never people. No "you are the problem", no shaming, no "lazy".
- Uplifting and shareable. End on hope plus one small action.

## Facts (LOCKED)
- Facts come only from pages actually opened during research (search, then open). Add a Source line.
- No invented numbers. No income or result promises. No "first-ever". No fake discounts.
- Never feature the brand's competitors. For SaaS Universe brands, that means no CRM, booking, inbox, email-marketing or WhatsApp tools.

## Rejected styles (do not use)
- Illustrated or drawn carousels.
- Photo-strip carousels (a stock photo on top with a small text card below).
- Use only the v3 white post-card and the v5 cream big-type, branded with the owner's name, photo and title.

## Topic Bank
- Keep at least 5 unused topics per slot. Each one gets a planned KEYWORD and a guide idea.
- Start from the brand's own best posts: anything above 2× the median reach becomes a topic or a "Part 2".
- Mark topics `USED <date> (<shortcode>)`.
